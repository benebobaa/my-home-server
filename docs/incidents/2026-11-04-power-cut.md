## Analysis

All three nodes and the router lost power within the same scrape interval
and booted within the same two seconds, with no shutdown in any journal. That
is a house power cut, not a fault in the lab. `RouterRebooted` fired once
monitoring was back, which is what the alert is for.

Recovery was fully automatic and took about 4 minutes from power to public
services (17:21 → 17:25). The order was the expected one: the node boots,
`pve-guests` starts the containers, the K3s VMs, then Postgres; the
Kubeletto API comes up last, because it needs both K3s and Postgres.

What came back hard but cleanly:

- **Postgres (CT 130)** ran crash recovery from WAL (2.4 MB, 0.07 s). No
  errors after.
- **The node filesystems** replayed their ext4 journals ("recovery complete",
  "1 orphan inode deleted" on pve2 and pve3). Nothing further was checked.
- **K3s** reported both nodes Ready. Tenant pods that Knative started during
  the boot were scaling back down (`Terminating`) at 17:26–17:30. One tenant's
  three crash-looping revisions (about 1,730 restarts) predate this incident
  and are tracked separately.

## Compared with the 2026-10-16 power cut

[2026-10-16](2026-10-16-power-cut-pve1-stuck-off.md) was a 10-minute cut
after which pve1 stayed off for three days. This time:

- **pve1 restored by itself** when power returned, two seconds after pve2 and
  pve3. Its BIOS restore-on-AC works when standby power is not stuck; the
  10-16 failure was the stuck standby, not the setting.
- **The laptops' clocks reset again** (dead CMOS cells), as recorded on
  10-16. chrony fixed them about 35 s after boot. Containers started in those
  35 s with an April clock; nothing visibly broke because of it.
- **The archive (CT 110) stayed off**, because it is still `onboot: 0`. This
  was another cold boot of pve2 with the archive's mount sources in fstab
  (`nofail`), and pve2 booted normally. `HostUnreachable` (warning) fires for
  the archive until it is started.

## Secondary findings

- **`HostTextfileCollectorStale` fires falsely after every power cut.** The
  SMART collector (and the apt one, which no alert watches) on pve2 and pve3
  runs at boot, before chrony steps the clock, so `smartmon.prom` gets a
  2026-04-14 mtime. The alert sees a
  file 200 days old and fires as a `warning`. It clears when the timers run
  again, 15 minutes after boot.
- **Nothing reported the outage while it lasted.** The monitoring stack and
  the hEX share the power. The healthchecks.io dead-man's switch is the only
  thing that can see a lab-wide cut. Whether it notified was not checked
  (also open from 10-16).
- **The outage was longer than it felt.** The operator estimated an hour; the
  evidence shows 2 h 20 min without power. Without a notification, the start
  of an outage is only known after the fact.
